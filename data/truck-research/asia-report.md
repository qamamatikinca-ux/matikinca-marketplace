# Asian truck reference research — 13 October 2026

All 89 assigned catalog family names are represented in asia.json. 52 have researched photographic reference candidates: 48 reusable Commons photographs/video stills and 4 manufacturer photographic references downloaded locally. Remaining 37 have null photo fields: no sufficiently verified model-specific reusable photograph was established. This is coverage of the existing family catalog, not a claim to include every truck model or year variant worldwide.

## Verified local photographs

- Hino 200: inspected official outdoor photograph, clear Hino 200 identification. Saved under public/images/truck-models/asia/hino-200.jpg.
- Isuzu FX-Series: inspected official quarry photograph, FXZ identification visible. Saved as isuzu-fx.jpg.
- FAW JH6: inspected official manufacturer news photograph of JH6 28.500FT fleet at FAW building. Saved as faw-jh6.jpg.
- FUSO FA / FI / FJ: inspected manufacturer photograph of an FJ mixer, genuine equipment detail on isolated white background. It is a representative FJ photo within the grouped family, not a claim that FA and FI have identical appearance. Saved as fuso-fj.jpg.

Manufacturer images are explicitly marked 'reuse permission not stated'. They must not be presented as Creative Commons assets. Hino's newer 700 hero image appeared heavily composited/possibly CGI and was rejected; the authentic Commons historical 700 photo was retained.

## Correctness boundaries

Every non-null photo has a model-specific source, caption and photographer/credit. Broad or ambiguous web hits were rejected: Tata Prima passenger-car concepts, Ashok Leyland buses, Eicher farming tractors, truck badge-only crops, unrelated BYD and Shacman derivatives. The BYD 8TT image is a licensed video still at 752×564; its original quality should not be overstated.

Photos describe model families and specific regional examples. They do not prove the selected year, engine, gearbox, axle or owner vehicle. Regional derivative labels remain in photoModelLabel. Explicit photo year bounds are provided only where sufficiently identified. Null bounds do not imply a match to every model year.

Defaults are conservative: 10 verified fuel values and 3 transmission values. Multiple family options or insufficient primary-source evidence leave defaults null. Official source references are attached only to reviewed relevant claims. Please retain editable confirmation fields rather than enforcing arbitrary broad family data as factory proof.

Hino 200 was introduced in South Africa in 2022 by rebadging the Toyota Dyna; catalog from=2010 is inaccurate for that South African name. Manufacturer engine designation '5L' is not five litres (current official 200 page lists 2986 cc). Xcient Fuel Cell manufacturer documentation specifies a 5-speed automatic; 'Electric direct drive' is not the appropriate default for it. BYD T5 now has hybrid variants, so family-wide Electric is unsafe. JH6, Pavise, Quester and Croner have multiple/changed gearbox offerings over the catalog's broad date range. Gas, diesel, electric or future derivative existence cannot be silently inferred from diesel-only catalog arrays.

## Retrieval limits

Direct Wikimedia shell downloads/API requests return HTTP 403 in this environment. Web open/click confirms indexed description pages and several upload image URLs, but returns text-only <<ImageDisplayed>> markers, with no ImageContent/base64 bytes. No Commons assets were falsely claimed as downloaded. Root should test the deterministic upload URL/image proxy and omit failed images cleanly. Manufacturer JPEG downloads succeeded and were visually inspected locally.

No application source, database or deployment was modified by this research task.
